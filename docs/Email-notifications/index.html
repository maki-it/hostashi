Email notifications¶

Hostashi can send invitations, security notes and notifications by email. It does not run a mail server: it hands every message to an SMTP relay you already have, such as your own mail server or a sending service.

Email is off until an admin sets up a relay. Without it everything works as before: the panel shows invitation links to copy, and problems show up on the overview and the Health page.

Set up the relay¶

For: admins.

  1. Open Email in the Administration part of the menu.
  2. Enter the relay:
  3. Host and Port of the SMTP server.
  4. Encryption: STARTTLS (usually port 587) or TLS (usually port 465). Hostashi never sends mail without encryption and always checks the server's certificate. For a relay with a certificate from your own CA, paste that CA under CA certificate (PEM).
  5. Username and Password, if the relay wants a login. Hostashi sends them only inside the encrypted connection and keeps the password encrypted with the server key. The page never shows it again; leave the field empty to keep it.
  6. From address and From name: the sender of every email, for example panel@example.com and Hostashi.
  7. Reply-to, optional.
  8. Save, then enter your own address under Send to and send a test email. The page shows the relay's answer, for example 535 5.7.8 authentication failed, if something is wrong.

Examples of relay settings (check your provider's documentation for the current values):

Relay Host Port Encryption
Your own mail server mail.example.com 587 STARTTLS
Amazon SES (eu-central-1) email-smtp.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com 587 STARTTLS
Brevo smtp-relay.brevo.com 587 STARTTLS
Postmark smtp.postmarkapp.com 587 STARTTLS

Deliverability is the relay's job. Set up SPF and DKIM for the sender domain at your provider, otherwise the emails may end up in spam. Hostashi does not sign messages itself.

Links in emails point to the panel's own address (the --panel-domain of the installer), never to whatever address a request came in on.

Your email address¶

For: every account.

On Your account → Email, enter your address. Hostashi sends a link to it; the address is used only after you open that link (within 24 hours). Changing the address sends a note to the old one. When an admin invites you with an address, accepting the invitation confirms it.

What Hostashi sends¶

Account emails go to your own confirmed address and cannot be switched off:

  • an invitation or a reset link from an admin,
  • a second factor added or removed, a passkey added or removed, a new API token,
  • your email address changed.

Notifications are about the server and sites. Switch them on or off on Your account → Notifications. They are on by default for admins and off for everyone else; Git deploys are off for everyone until switched on. You only get notifications about what your role can see: backups and server checks only go to admins; managers get those of every site, users those of their own sites.

Notification When
Backups A backup failed or did not save everything. Once per run.
Certificates A renewal failed (at most once a day), or a certificate expires in 14 or in 3 days.
Server checks Once a day, when the checks on the Health page find errors. Also once per new Hostashi version, when the server does not install it by itself (--auto-update).
Services A service is down (systemd gave up restarting it) or crashed 3 times within 15 minutes, or the panel cannot reach the Hostashi agent for about 3 minutes. Another email when it runs again. Server services go to admins; PHP and the object cache of a site also to managers and users of that site.
Malware scan A malware scan of a site flagged files it had not reported before. Once per run, with up to 10 paths and signatures; files you marked as false positives are not reported again.
Git deploys A Git deploy of a site failed: its build, its switch to the new release, or its start. Once per run, with the commit, who started it and the error; the log stays in the panel. A failed rollback sends none, as the panel shows it right away.
PHP versions Once a week per site, when a site uses a PHP version that loses security fixes within 60 days or already has.
Disk space A disk of the server or a site's disk limit is 90 percent full or more, or a site's databases reach 90 percent of their warning size. At most once a day.

Language¶

Emails are plain text in English or German. Each recipient gets the language set under Your account → Appearance → Language; Follow the browser means English, because an email has no browser to follow. An invitation goes to an account that has no setting yet, so it is in the language of the admin who sent it. Times are in the server's time zone (Server → System). The texts of the server checks are translated too, in the disk space, database size, PHP version and Hostashi update emails as in the daily list of failed checks. Error messages from programs such as restic, nginx or the ACME client stay in English.

At most 10 emails per recipient and 100 per server go out each hour. Above that, notifications wait for the next hour; account emails are always sent.

Hostashi itself sends these emails, so it cannot report an outage of the panel, of the whole server or of its network. Use a check from outside for that, for example an uptime monitor that opens your sites.

When email does not arrive¶

  • The Health page and the overview show Email with a warning when messages failed in the last 24 hours, with the relay's last answer.
  • Hostashi tries again after 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes and then every hour or two, for up to 24 hours. A permanent rejection (a 5xx answer, for example an unknown recipient) is not retried.
  • Send a test email on the Email page to see the relay's answer right away.
  • Invitation and reset links are always shown on the Users page as well, so you can copy them by hand.

Email is not a secure channel: anyone with access to a mailbox can open an invitation link in it. Links work once and only for 24 hours, and admins and managers still need a second factor.
